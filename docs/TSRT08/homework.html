In assessing the homework assignments, the following rules apply:

  • Submit solutions to the teaching assistant before the corresponding deadline. Send the solution as a PDF to seyyed.reza.jafari@liu.se.
  • Work in groups of two students. If you do not have a partner, email the teaching assistant.
  • Group discussions are allowed, but solutions, calculations, and code must not be copied.
  • Both students in a group must be able to explain the solution they submit.
  • All students must be prepared to give the teaching assistant a short oral review of their solution individually; students do not necessarily have to attend as a group.
  • Assignments are graded pass, fail, or resubmit. A resubmission must be handed in within one week. If the next submission is also graded resubmit, the following submission date is at the next examination occasion for the course.
