This manuscript deals with the informativity and identifiability
conditions for network of dynamic systems. The authors focus their
analysis using the so-called Grobner basis and provide graph-theoretic
conditions. For informatitvity, sufficient conditions are derived in
terms of the rank of a matrix of rational functions, relating the
excitation signals to the inputs of the optimal predictor. The
identifiability is investigated under the lens of  the dimensionality
of a  proper fiber for the local identifiability. The paper assumes
full excitation of the network, but partial measurement. This paper
also provides an example under which it states that local
identifiability does not imply generic identifiability, a hypothesis
that was previously discussed in the literature.

This paper is clear and well-written and there is also a clear
contribution to the field, but it is hard to follow specially because
the authors tend to omit key steps. Therefore, I have some concerns
about the results of the paper given in the following.

1. The network model used in this manuscript assumes that P is
invertible for well-posedness. No remarks nor references are given to
state under what conditions this transfer matrix is invertible.
    
2. There are no formal proofs for none of the Theorems presented in the
informativity section. The ideas behind the proofs are simple given in
informal terms, and mostly they are cited as generalizations of works
from other.  I recommend inserting formal proofs for all the results
stating exactly which are necessary, instead of just referring to a
book covering a wide topic.  
    
3. While the characterization is given in terms of input to predictors
input, how exactly one knows which predictors to use? There are several
ways to select the predictors.
    
4. Does one need to assume that the topology of the network is known?
This assumption is not stated in the manuscript.
    
5. Theorem 3 seems interesting by its own, but it is not clear how to
construct the Gronber basis from it.
    
6. The same comment applies to Theorem 4, which derives a necessary and
sufficient condition for local identifiability.
